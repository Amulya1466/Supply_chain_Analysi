# 📦 Supply Chain Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing supply chain operations to evaluate shipment performance, delivery efficiency, shipping costs, discounts, and order fulfillment trends.

An interactive Power BI dashboard was developed to transform raw supply chain data into meaningful business insights, helping stakeholders identify operational inefficiencies, monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), and support data-driven decision-making.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Monitor overall shipment and order performance.
- Analyze on-time and late shipment trends.
- Evaluate shipping performance across different shipping modes.
- Understand the impact of discount ranges on order and shipment patterns.
- Identify operational bottlenecks and potential areas for improvement.
- Support better supply chain planning and decision-making.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Power BI** – Dashboard development and data visualization.
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation.
- **DAX** – Calculated measures and KPI development.
- **Excel** – Dataset management and initial data preparation.

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard includes key metrics such as:

- Total Shipments
- On-Time Shipments
- Late Shipments
- On-Time Delivery Rate
- Late Shipment Rate

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

The interactive dashboard explores the following areas:

1. **Shipment Performance Analysis** – Evaluates overall shipment and delivery performance.
2. **Shipping Mode Analysis** – Compares shipment patterns across different transportation modes.
3. **Discount Analysis** – Examines shipment distribution across discount ranges.
4. **Delivery Status Analysis** – Highlights on-time and late shipment trends.
5. **Operational Insights** – Identifies areas where supply chain efficiency can be improved.

## 💡 Business Insights

The analysis helps answer important business questions:

- What percentage of shipments are delivered on time?
- Which shipping modes account for the highest shipment volumes?
- How are shipments distributed across different discount ranges?
- What proportion of shipments experience delivery delays?
- Where can operational improvements help enhance delivery performance?

## 🚀 Business Recommendations

- Monitor late shipment trends regularly to identify recurring delivery issues.
- Evaluate shipping mode performance to improve transportation planning.
- Investigate operational factors contributing to delivery delays.
- Monitor discount patterns alongside shipment performance.
- Establish regular KPI tracking to support continuous improvement.
